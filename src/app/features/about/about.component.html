About

A Taliferro Tech product · Part of TODD

Describe it. TODD creates it.

TODD Image Creator turns a plain-language description into a downloadable image — a logo, an icon, a banner — right in the conversation. Attach a reference image or an existing logo and TODD works from that instead.

What it is

A conversation, not a form.

Tell TODD what you want. If anything's unclear, TODD asks before generating — no prompt-engineering, no settings panel to learn.

Category

AI image generation.

TODD Image Creator belongs to the design and image-generation category — logos, icons, and banners generated from text or an attached reference image.

Who made it

Taliferro Tech, LLC.

TODD Image Creator is made by Taliferro Tech and is part of TODD, the Momentum System. Taliferro Tech creates connected software products that help people move from information to action.

Privacy

Nothing is saved.

Images exist for the conversation only. Download anything you want to keep before you leave — there's no history or gallery to come back to.
